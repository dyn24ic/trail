# trAIl / TrailGuardian Context

## Product Name
trAIl (Trail Guardian) – Note: Capital "I" and lowercase "l" are distinct.

## Category
Search & Rescue, Outdoor Safety, AI-enabled Hiking Assistance

## Mission
Provide a **cost-effective, AI-powered system** to detect, locate, and respond to lost or injured hikers while generating actionable insights to improve trail safety. The system leverages **drones, sensors, thermal imaging, audio detection, and AI prediction models** to minimize response times and prevent accidents.

---

## MVP Overview

### Stage 1: Mapping & Sensor Placement
- Use **drones** to survey trail networks (GPS paths, elevation, water crossings, exposure points, access points, cell dead zones).  
- AI risk model scores hotspots based on historical incidents, terrain difficulty, remoteness, and weather exposure.  
- Deploy **low-cost stationary sensors ($80)** at high-risk points and **emergency call boxes ($150)** at trailheads and major junctions.  

### Stage 2: Accident Detection & Drone Response
- **Trigger events:** emergency call box press, sensor anomaly (motion or audio), overdue hikers, or 911 input.  
- **AI predicts search zones** using historical data, terrain, and environmental conditions.  
- **Drone search:** thermal and visual imaging, grid search of top 3 likely zones, GPS beacon and live feed if victim detected.  
- **AI injury assessment:** quick triage based on motion, posture, thermal signature, audio, and visible injuries.  

### Stage 2b: Responder Route Optimization
- AI calculates **fastest and safest route** for ground or helicopter responders considering distance, elevation, weather, obstacles, and injury severity.  
- Mobile app provides **victim location, ETA, route hazards, and turn-by-turn guidance**.

### Stage 3: Post-Mortem & Trail Improvement
- Automated **incident reports** with timeline, root cause analysis, environmental conditions, and historical context.  
- AI generates **recommendations**: warning signs, temporary closures, trail reroutes, sensor optimization, and dynamic risk alerts.  
- Continuous **learning system**: adjusts trail difficulty ratings, personal risk scores, and predictive warnings based on aggregated incident data.

---

## Key MVP Features
- Real-time detection of hiker emergencies via **motion, audio, and overdue tracking**.  
- **Drone-based rapid search** using AI-predicted zones.  
- **Automated injury triage** and responder routing.  
- **Post-incident analytics** to improve trail safety.  
- Low-cost, scalable sensor network and emergency infrastructure.  
- System **learns over time** to optimize search, risk alerts, and prevention.

## Target Users / Stakeholders
- Search & Rescue teams / park authorities  
- Outdoor adventure organizations  
- Hikers using remote or high-risk trails  
- Trail managers interested in safety and preventive analytics

---

## Core Value Proposition
- **Fast:** Locate victims in 5-15 minutes vs 30-90 min traditional search.  
- **Cheap:** ~$2k setup per 20km trail, ~$1k annual operation.  
- **Smart:** AI predicts likely zones, optimizes responder deployment, and generates actionable trail safety recommendations.  
- **Learning & preventive:** Continuously improves system accuracy, alerts, and trail safety interventions.
